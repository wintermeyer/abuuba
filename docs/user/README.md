# User guide

For people with an account on an abuuba server. Each page is one task, in the
order most people meet them.

1. **[Getting started](getting-started.md)** — signing up, confirming your
   address, and what to do in the first ten minutes.
2. **[Posting](posting.md)** — writing, content warnings, who can see a post,
   polls, media, scheduling.
3. **[Your timeline](timelines.md)** — following people, what the home feed
   shows, and how to change what it shows.
4. **[Finding things](finding-things.md)** — explore, the directory, hashtags
   and search.
5. **[Profiles](profiles.md)** — reading somebody's profile and acting on it.
6. **[Notifications](notifications.md)** — the column, the policy for who can
   reach you, and the waiting list.
7. **[Safety](safety.md)** — blocking, muting, filters, reporting, and two-step
   sign-in.
8. **[Settings](settings.md)** — every switch, in the order the settings pages
   present them.
9. **[Public pages, embeds and sharing](public-pages.md)** — what people
   without an account see.
