# Getting started

## Signing up

Go to `/register`. You will be asked to read the server's rules first, because
they are what moderation goes by and agreeing to them without reading them is
how people end up surprised.

Then four things: a username, an email address, a password of at least twelve
characters, and a tick to say you agree to the rules. A sentence you can
remember makes a better password than a short jumble.

Depending on how the server is run, you may also see:

- **A puzzle**, if the server asks new accounts to prove they are a person.
- **A "why would you like to join?" box**, if accounts here are approved by
  hand. A moderator reads what you write, so a sentence about who you are does
  more than a full stop.
- **"Registrations are closed"**, if the server is not taking anybody right
  now. Some servers still accept invitation links when they are closed to the
  public.

You can also sign up from inside a Mastodon app rather than in the browser. The
app asks the same things and the account it makes is the same account; a server
that asks new accounts to solve a puzzle is the one case an app cannot do,
because it has no puzzle to show you, and there you have to use `/register`.

## Confirming your address

You will get an email with a link. Following it confirms the address is yours.

If the server approves accounts by hand, that is a separate step and it happens
after the confirmation: you get a second mail when a moderator lets you in.
Nothing is wrong if that takes a day.

If the mail does not arrive, or you typed the address wrong, ask the app that
signed you up to send it again — it can correct the address at the same time.
Only that app can: another one has no business sending you mail. In the browser,
sign up again with the right address.

## Your username, and the other one

Your username is what you picked. Your **address** is that plus the server:
`@alice@example.com`, the same shape as an email address and for the same
reason. It is what somebody on another server types to find you, and it is
printed on your profile.

You cannot change your username later. Everything published under it — every
post, every follow — is named after it, on servers you do not control.

## The first ten minutes

1. **Write something.** A profile with no posts gives nobody a reason to
   follow it. See [posting](posting.md).
2. **Fill in your profile.** A display name and a couple of lines about
   yourself, at `/settings/profile`, plus up to four fields that take a label
   and a value each — which is where most people put links. An avatar and a
   header image are not in the web interface yet.
3. **Follow ten people.** Your home timeline is exactly the people you follow
   and nothing else, so it is empty until you do. [Finding
   things](finding-things.md) covers explore, the directory, hashtags and
   search.
4. **Decide who can reach you**, if you want to. The defaults are reasonable;
   [safety](safety.md) covers changing them.

## If you came from another server

If you have an account elsewhere and want to move here, do it from the old
account: it tells its followers where you went, and they follow along. The
walk-through is in [settings](settings.md#moving-to-another-account) — start it
before you delete anything, because a deleted account cannot point anywhere.
