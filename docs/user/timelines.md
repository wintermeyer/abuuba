# Your timeline

## What home shows

`/home` is the people you follow, newest first, and nothing else. No algorithm
decides the order and nothing is inserted because it was popular. An empty home
timeline means you have not followed anybody yet, not that nothing is
happening.

Boosts appear too: somebody you follow passing on a post from somebody you do
not. That is how most people find the next person to follow.

## New posts wait for you

A post arriving while you are reading would push what you are reading down the
screen, and on a busy day it would do that every few seconds. So arriving posts
become a count at the top — **3 new posts** — and go in when you press it.

If you are already at the top, they go straight in, because that is what you
would expect there.

## Following somebody

Press **Follow** on their profile. Their posts start appearing in your home
timeline from that moment; what they posted before is on their profile, not
retroactively in your feed.

If they have asked to approve their followers, the button becomes a request and
their posts arrive once they accept. You will not be told if they never do.

**Unfollowing** takes them out of your timeline, including the posts of theirs
already in it. So does blocking.

## What never reaches home, and why

Some things are decided when a post is written and some when you read it, which
is why unblocking somebody does not bring back what you missed while they were
blocked.

Filtered out as the post arrives:

- anybody either of you has blocked, and anybody on a blocked server
- somebody you had already muted
- posts in a language you have filtered out for that person
- boosts from somebody whose boosts you have turned off
- replies to people you do not follow — a reply reaches you only if you follow
  the person being replied to as well, or it is somebody replying to their own
  post, or the reply is to you

Filtered out as you read:

- blocks, mutes and muted threads as they stand right now
- a post whose author has since made it more private

## Settings that belong to one follow

On the profile of somebody you follow, under **What you see from them**:

- **Their boosts of other people** can be turned off while you keep following
  them, for somebody whose own posts you want and whose taste in boosts you do
  not.
- **Only these languages** narrows a follow to the languages you read, for
  somebody who posts in more than one. The list offers the languages that
  account actually posts in, and choosing none of them means all of them.
- **Tell me when they post** adds their posts to your
  [notifications](notifications.md) as well as your timeline, for somebody you
  do not want to miss. Their boosts and their replies to other people are not
  announced.

Both take effect on posts that arrive afterwards. Following, unfollowing,
muting and blocking a person are on the same profile, and the word filters that
work across every timeline are in [settings](settings.md) and described in
[safety](safety.md).

## Muting a thread

A conversation you are in that keeps going without you is a notification every
few minutes. The **…** menu on a post has **Mute this conversation**, which
stops those and takes the thread out of your timeline. Nothing is deleted and
nobody is told, and the same menu unmutes it.

It is on the menu of a post that is part of a thread — a post nobody has
replied to and that replies to nothing has no conversation to mute. You will
find it on your home timeline and on the post's own page.
